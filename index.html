Fishing Regulations

Provo River — reference guide

Always verify current rules before fishing. Regulations change annually. Official source: Utah DWR — wildlife.utah.gov

License Requirements

  • All anglers age 12 and older must carry a valid Utah fishing license.
  • Combination and fishing-only licenses available at utahonline.utah.gov or most sporting goods retailers.
  • Non-resident licenses available online or in person at license agents statewide.

Middle Provo — Jordanelle to Deer Creek

Catch & Release
Artificial Only
  • Trout: catch and release only — all sizes must be returned.
  • Artificial lures and flies only; bait fishing is prohibited.
  • Designated Blue Ribbon fishery — one of Utah's premier tailwater trout rivers.

Lower Provo — Below Deer Creek Dam

Artificial Only
  • Artificial lures and flies only through key Provo Canyon reaches.
  • Slot and size limits for this section vary year to year — verify before fishing.
  • Special regulations extend through canyon sections near the reservoir outlet.

Upper Provo

  • Standard regulations apply to most upper reaches above Jordanelle Reservoir.
  • Some tributaries have spring closures to protect spawning fish.
  • Confirm whether specific pools carry artificial-only requirements in the current year.

Bag & Size Limits

  • Catch and release sections: no bag limit — all fish must be released unharmed.
  • Standard Utah trout limit is 4 fish per day where general regulations apply.
  • Check for current slot size limits on Brown Trout on applicable sections.

Seasonal Notes

  • The Provo River is open year-round on most designated special-regulation sections.
  • Deer Creek and Jordanelle Reservoirs follow separate reservoir-specific rules.
  • High-elevation tributaries may have spring closures — always check headwater rules before fishing.
